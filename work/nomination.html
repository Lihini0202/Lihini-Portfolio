Choosing who manages your data

India's DPDP Act lets people nominate someone to manage their personal data if they die or can't act. I designed and built this flow for WSO2's open-source consent portal during my internship.

The problem

A legal process most people have never heard of

DPDP Act §14 gives people the right to nominate someone for their data. The law is clear, but the process is unfamiliar and a little uncomfortable to think about. The flow had to feel safe, explain itself, and work for people who are not technical.

Who it is for

Two people in one flow

  • The user choosing a nominee and deciding what they can do.
  • The nominee who later needs to act on that person's behalf, securely.
What I did

Design grounded in real precedent

  • Studied how Facebook and Google handle similar features, like legacy contacts.
  • Designed the flow in Figma, from choosing a nominee to confirming access.
  • Built it into the open-source platform and got it through maintainer code review.
  • Made sure it worked across the platform's 23 languages.

Key decisions

Why it looks the way it does

One decision per step

Splitting the flow into short steps with a progress bar keeps a serious decision from feeling overwhelming.

Plain language over legal terms

Each step says what will happen in everyday words, with the legal reference available but not in the way.

Clear confirmation at the end

Users see exactly who they nominated and what that person can do, so nothing is left to guess.
